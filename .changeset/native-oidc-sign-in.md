Add native Sign in with Apple for any number of iOS and macOS apps, from Swift or Expo: declare `Apple.provider({ kind: "native", bundleIds })` without a signing key and expose `AuthContract.oauthNativeSignIn` and `AuthContract.oauthCompleteNativeSignIn`. `Oidc.makeVerifier` accepts several ID-token `audiences`.
