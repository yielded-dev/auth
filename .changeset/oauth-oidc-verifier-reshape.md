Reshape `@yielded/oauth/Oidc` with key-based `verify`, key-set `verifyWithKeySet`, a `Discovery`-backed `Verifier` service (`makeVerifier`, `makeVerifierRemote`, and layers), `IssuerPolicy`, and `fetchUserInfo`. Move verify-time Microsoft Entra and LINE HS256 handling into `@yielded/auth`, enforce pinned Entra tenant GUID matching on signed `tid` claims, and anchor `max_age` freshness checks to flow `requestedAt`.

BEHAVIOR CHANGE: Direct `@yielded/oauth` consumers must migrate from `Oidc.makeVerifier({ metadata, ... })` to `Oidc.verify`, `Oidc.verifyWithKeySet`, `Oidc.makeVerifier`, or `Oidc.makeVerifierRemote` over `Discovery`, moving advertised metadata capability checks and provider-specific issuer policies (such as Entra `{tenantid}` and per-key `issuer`) to caller configuration. Across `@yielded/oauth` and `@yielded/auth`, ID-token verification now defaults `clockTolerance` to `"30 seconds"`, widens `@yielded/oauth` default asymmetric algorithms to all `effect/jose` asymmetric algorithms (`RS*`, `PS*`, `ES*`, `EdDSA`), validates protected-header `typ` immediately after signature verification (accepting omitted `typ` or `"JWT"` / `"application/jwt"` by default), enforces multi-audience `aud`/`azp` policy and refresh `aud` continuity, verifies `at_hash` and `c_hash` only when `accessToken` or `code` is supplied unless `requireCodeHash: true`, fixes `max_age: 0` with `>= 1s` login delay and refresh `auth_time` when the prior token omitted `auth_time`, and maps initial authorization-code ID-token post-signature rejections and UserInfo `subjectMismatch` (a valid `sub` differing from the verified ID token) to `OAuthProtocolRejected` while keeping missing/malformed UserInfo `sub` (`InvalidResponse`) and connected refresh post-exchange failures `OAuthUnavailable`. Existing stored connected grants require no reset or reconnection.
