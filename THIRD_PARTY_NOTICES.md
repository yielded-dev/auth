# Third-party notices

Yielded credits the open-source projects and independent standards behind its
cryptography. The [crypto package notices](packages/crypto/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
record the exact source revisions, authors, licenses, affected files, and the
adapted Noble, Wycheproof, Argon2 and standards test material. That file is
included in the published `@yielded/crypto` package.

The existing `@yielded/auth-crypto` adapters also use Paul Miller's MIT-licensed
[noble-hashes](https://github.com/paulmillr/noble-hashes) and
[noble-ciphers](https://github.com/paulmillr/noble-ciphers). Their versions are
owned by the root catalog; the libraries ship their own license notices.
